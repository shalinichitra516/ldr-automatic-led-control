  
**LDR BASED AUTOMATIC LED CONTROL**

**AIM**

To design and implement a simple circuit using an LDR, NPN transistor and LED to automatically control the LED based on the surrounding light intensity.

**COMPONENTS REQUIRED**

1. LDR (Light Dependent Resistor)  
2. NPN Transistor  
3. LED  
4. Resistors – 1 kΩ  
5. 9V Battery  
6. Breadboard  
7. Connecting wires

**WORKING PRINCIPLE**

1. The circuit works based on the change in resistance of the LDR according to the surrounding light.  
2. In bright light, the resistance of the LDR decreases.  
3. In low light/darkness, the resistance of the LDR increases.  
4. This change controls the NPN transistor.  
5. The transistor acts as a switch and controls the LED.  
6. Thus, the LED can be automatically controlled according to the light level.

**CIRCUIT CONNECTION**

The LDR and resistor form the sensing section of the circuit. The output of this section is connected to the base of the NPN transistor. The transistor controls the current through the LED and its resistor.

**PROCEDURE**

1. Connect the components on the breadboard according to the circuit diagram.  
2. Connect the 9V battery to the circuit.  
3. Place the LDR where it can sense the surrounding light.  
4. Observe the LED under bright light and low-light conditions.  
5. Cover the LDR with your hand and observe the change in the LED.  
6. Verify the automatic switching operation.

**RESULT**

The LDR-based automatic LED control circuit was successfully constructed and tested. The LED operation changes according to the light intensity detected by the LDR.

**APPLICATION**

1. Automatic street lights  
2. Night lamps  
3. Light-sensitive security systems  
4. Automatic lighting systems  
5. Energy-saving lighting systems

**CONCLUTION**

The project demonstrates how an LDR and transistor can be used to automatically control an LED based on surrounding light intensity. It provides a simple example of a light-sensing and automatic switching circuit.
